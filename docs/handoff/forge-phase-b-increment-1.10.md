# Forge Phase B — Increment 1.10 Handoff

## Delivered

Engineering Mission Model 1.10 establishes Forge's immutable, local,
non-executing highest operational grouping artifact. It captures a Mission's
objective, explicit boundaries, ordered revision-pinned Engineering Intent
memberships, dependencies, declarative derived progress, aggregate evidence,
closed lifecycle, and completion requirements.

## Boundaries

Engineering Intent remains the canonical artifact for one bounded increment
under Constitution Article 3. A Mission groups Intents but does not revise,
approve, execute, persist, or schedule them. Runtime Prompt Generation stays
per-Intent, and Runtime Providers remain future consumers only. No Mission
Runtime, queue, scheduler, AI planning, Studio, repository operation, or
execution was added.

## Completion

Mission completion requires every exact member Intent to be `VERIFIED` or
`ARCHIVED`, plus reproducible repository, validation, and constitutional
compliance evidence. The model does not retrieve or assess evidence itself.

## Recommended next increment

Forge Phase B — Increment 1.11 — Mission Planning should establish only
declarative Mission planning and sequencing assessment. It must preserve the
Mission Model's immutable, local, non-executing boundary.
