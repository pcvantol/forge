records why Forge exists, its governed engineering lifecycle, human authority,
intended product responsibilities, explicit non-goals, bootstrap philosophy,

The Vision is deliberately distinct from the
[Forge Constitution](../../knowledge/bootstrap/01_CONSTITUTION.md): the
Constitution defines permanent principles and authority boundaries, while the
Vision explains the enduring product outcome those principles serve.
