# Forge Solution Template Framework Architecture Authoring Report 001

Solution Templates are immutable, versioned catalogue assets. Their deterministic
draft generator is deliberately upstream of Business Review and records template
provenance without fabricating Architecture Review or Mission Recommendation
evidence. This preserves the authoritative Business Workspace and Architecture
Workspace boundaries and makes required-discipline gaps explicit rather than
inventing expertise.

The framework is local, provider-independent, and advisory. It introduces no
automatic approval, Mission execution, Execution Host, planner, marketplace,
or organisational template sharing.
