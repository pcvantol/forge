# Forge engineering method

Generic engineering-workflow mechanics are governed by the generated
[AI-development projection](docs/ai-development/GENERATED_PROJECTION.md).
This file records only Forge product authority.

Forge's canonical Mission lifecycle and Mission Intake boundary are in
[Mission-driven engineering](docs/architecture/engineering-mission.md).
Business and Architecture approvals are product governance in the
[Business Workspace](docs/architecture/business-workspace.md) and
[Architecture Workspace](docs/architecture/architecture-workspace.md).
Forge derives Mission planning, Engineering Actions and Producer Contracts;
the installed Execution Host owns execution, host qualification, telemetry and
execution evidence. The versioned boundary is the
[Execution Host Contract](docs/architecture/execution-host-contract.md) and
[Producer Contract](docs/architecture/producer-contract.md).

This local method does not grant approval, allocate Missions, or make Forge an
Engineering Platform, Workspace or TDE authority.
