MISSION-0001 — Autonomous Engineering Foundation

Status: APPROVED_FOR_ARCHITECTURE
Portfolio: Forge Core Platform
Owner: Business
Priority: Highest
Mission Type: Portfolio Seed
---

Mission Statement

Forge shall become capable of autonomously executing approved engineering Missions within explicitly approved governance boundaries.
The outcome of this Mission is the first operational autonomous engineering loop.
Forge must be capable of receiving a Mission, executing bounded engineering work through an Execution Host, processing execution evidence, and completing the Mission without manual prompt authoring.
This Mission establishes the engineering foundation upon which all future Forge capabilities depend.
---

Business Objective

Reduce engineering orchestration overhead.
Remove manual prompt authoring.
Provide deterministic engineering execution.
Establish Forge as an AI-native engineering platform.
---

Business Value

Very High
This capability enables every subsequent Forge capability.
Without autonomous engineering the remaining roadmap cannot become self-hosting.
---

Success Criteria

Forge can:
- accept an approved Mission;
- perform Mission Intake;
- execute Engineering Actions autonomously;
- communicate only through the Execution Host Contract;
- use Engineering Platform 1.5 as the temporary Execution Host;
- process Execution Evidence;
- resume deterministically after interruption;
- complete the Mission autonomously.
No manual prompt authoring is required after Mission approval.
---

In Scope

The Mission may include:
- Mission Intake
- Forge CLI
- Mission Runner
- Mission Scheduler
- Mission State Store
- Runtime Prompt Renderer
- Bootstrap Execution Host Adapter
- End-to-End Mission Canary
- Execution qualification
Only capabilities required to establish autonomous engineering execution.
---

Out of Scope

This Mission explicitly excludes:
- Forge Studio
- Portfolio UI
- Business Workspace
- Architecture Workspace
- AI Mission Planner
- Mission Recommendation Engine
- Additional AI providers
- Cloud execution
- Multiple Execution Hosts
These belong to future Missions.
---

Governance

Mission Objectives may not change during execution.
Architecture changes require explicit approval.
Forge may generate Engineering Intents.
Forge may generate Engineering Actions.
Forge may not change Mission scope.

Mission Completion

This Mission completes only when:
An approved Mission can be executed autonomously through Engineering Platform 1.5 without manual prompt creation.
Execution Evidence proves deterministic completion.
Mission State survives interruption and resume.
Execution remains bounded by the approved Mission.
---

Expected Portfolio Outcome

Upon completion Forge becomes capable of executing approved engineering Missions autonomously.
Future Portfolio Seeds may then focus on:
- Architecture intelligence
- Portfolio intelligence
- Forge Studio
- Business governance
- AI ecosystem
rather than execution infrastructure.
---

Business Approval Rationale

Autonomous engineering execution is the foundational capability required before higher-level portfolio management, business workflows and architectural intelligence can provide operational value.
Approving this Mission authorizes Forge to become operational as an engineering platform, while preserving explicit human governance over Mission approval and strategic direction.
