MISSION-0002 — Architectural Intelligence Foundation

Status: APPROVED_FOR_ARCHITECTURE
Portfolio: Forge Core Platform
Owner: Business
Priority: Highest
Mission Type: Portfolio Seed
---

Mission Statement

Forge shall become capable of continuously evaluating its own architecture and engineering progress using repository knowledge and execution evidence.
The outcome of this Mission is an Architectural Intelligence capability that can assess repository maturity, identify implementation pressure, recommend future engineering work and produce evidence-based Mission Recommendations.
Forge shall advise.
Humans remain responsible for strategic approval.
---

Business Objective

Reduce dependency on manual architectural review.
Provide continuous architectural insight.
Enable evidence-based engineering planning.
Create a self-improving engineering platform.
---

Business Value

Very High
Autonomous engineering has limited value without autonomous architectural reasoning.
This Mission enables Forge to become an engineering advisor rather than merely an execution engine.
---

Success Criteria

Forge can:
- review repository state;
- consume Engineering Reports;
- consume Execution Evidence;
- evaluate implementation maturity;
- detect architectural pressure;
- identify implementation gaps;
- recommend the next engineering increment;
- generate Mission Recommendations;
- explain recommendation rationale;
- quantify recommendation confidence.
Recommendations remain advisory.
Humans approve Missions.
---

In Scope

This Mission may include:
- Architecture Review Engine
- Repository Maturity Assessment
- Mission Recommendation Engine
- Recommendation Confidence Model
- Portfolio reasoning
- Architecture evidence model
- Recommendation reports
Only capabilities required to establish architectural intelligence.
---

Out of Scope

This Mission explicitly excludes:
- autonomous Mission approval;
- Business Workspace;
- Forge Studio;
- AI Business Advisor;
- cloud execution;
- additional Execution Hosts;
- commercial reasoning;
- legal reasoning;
- customer research.
These belong to future Missions.
---

Governance

Forge may recommend Missions.
Forge may not approve Missions.
Forge may not alter Portfolio objectives.
Mission approval remains the responsibility of the Business Owner.
Architecture approval remains the responsibility of the Platform Architect.
---

Required Disciplines

Platform Architecture
Engineering
Repository Analysis
Software Quality
No external commercial or legal disciplines required.

Expected Deliverables

Architecture Review Engine
Mission Recommendation Engine
Repository Maturity Model
Recommendation Reports
Mission Recommendation artefact
Recommendation confidence model
Updated Architecture Handbook
Canonical documentation
---

Dependencies

MISSION-0001 — Autonomous Engineering Foundation

---

Mission Completion

This Mission completes only when Forge can independently generate evidence-based Mission Recommendations from repository state and execution evidence without relying on historical engineering conversations.
Recommendations remain advisory.
Mission approval remains human.
---

Expected Portfolio Outcome

Upon completion Forge becomes capable of advising the Business Owner and Platform Architect on the next engineering priorities.
Future Portfolio Seeds may then focus on:
- Portfolio Governance
- Forge Studio
- AI Business Advisor
- Multi-provider AI ecosystem
rather than core architectural reasoning.
---

Business Approval Rationale

The value of autonomous engineering increases significantly when Forge can also determine which engineering opportunities are most valuable.
This Mission enables continuous architectural learning while preserving explicit human governance over strategic direction and Mission approval.
