MISSION-0005 — Multi-Disciplinary Intelligence Foundation

Status: APPROVED_FOR_ARCHITECTURE
Portfolio: Forge Core Platform
Owner: Business
Priority: High
Mission Type: Portfolio Seed
---

Mission Statement

Forge shall evolve from a software engineering platform into a multidisciplinary AI-native Product Development Platform.
Forge must become capable of combining expertise from multiple professional disciplines while preserving explicit human governance.
Engineering remains one discipline.
Forge shall recognise when additional expertise is required and coordinate the appropriate AI Advisors.
---

Business Objective

Enable better strategic decisions.
Improve Mission quality before engineering begins.
Reduce rework by involving relevant disciplines before implementation.
Support complete product development rather than engineering alone.
---

Business Value

Very High
Forge becomes capable of supporting the complete product lifecycle instead of software implementation only.
This significantly increases the value of every Mission.
---

Success Criteria

Forge supports multiple advisor disciplines.
Forge can:
- identify required disciplines;
- identify missing expertise;
- coordinate multiple Advisors;
- combine recommendations;
- expose confidence per discipline;
- explain conflicting advice.
Forge remains evidence-based.
Forge never fabricates expertise.

Business Approval Rationale

The greatest value of Forge is not autonomous engineering.
It is enabling better product decisions by combining multiple disciplines through evidence-based AI while preserving clear governance and human accountability.
This Mission establishes the long-term intelligence foundation of Forge.
