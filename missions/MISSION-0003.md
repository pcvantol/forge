MISSION-0003 — Forge Studio Foundation

Status: APPROVED_FOR_ARCHITECTURE
Portfolio: Forge Core Platform
Owner: Business
Priority: High
Mission Type: Portfolio Seed
---

Mission Statement

Forge shall provide a unified AI-native workspace where Business Owners, Platform Architects and Engineering Execution interact with the same portfolio while maintaining clearly separated responsibilities.
Forge Studio becomes the primary user experience for Forge.
Forge Studio is not an engineering runtime.
Forge Studio orchestrates governance, planning, review and execution visibility.
Execution remains the responsibility of the Forge Runtime.
---

Business Objective

Create a single operational environment for AI-assisted product development.
Reduce context switching.
Provide role-based workspaces.
Enable transparent governance across the complete capability lifecycle.
---

Business Value

Very High
Forge Studio becomes the operational face of Forge.
Business, Architecture and Engineering collaborate inside one platform while remaining separated by governance boundaries.
---

Success Criteria

Forge Studio provides:
- Business Workspace
- Architecture Workspace
- Execution Workspace
- Analytics Workspace
Each workspace exposes only the responsibilities appropriate for its role.
Forge Studio never performs engineering directly.
Forge Studio orchestrates Forge Runtime.
---

- Engineering analytics

Mission Completion

This Mission completes only when Forge Studio provides a unified role-based experience that supports the complete capability lifecycle while preserving explicit governance boundaries.
Forge Studio remains an orchestration layer.
Engineering execution continues to be performed by Forge Runtime.
---

Upon completion Forge becomes usable as a complete product development platform.
Future Missions may focus on:
- Business Advisor

- Cloud Runtime
- Portfolio intelligence

---

Business Approval Rationale

Forge has matured beyond an engineering engine.
A unified Studio enables Business, Architecture and Engineering to collaborate through AI while preserving clear ownership, governance and accountability.
Forge Studio therefore becomes the canonical operational interface for Forge without assuming ownership of engineering execution.
