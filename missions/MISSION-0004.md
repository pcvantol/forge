MISSION-0004 — Portfolio Governance Foundation

Status: APPROVED_FOR_ARCHITECTURE
Portfolio: Forge Core Platform
Owner: Business
Priority: High
Mission Type: Portfolio Seed
---

Mission Statement

Forge shall provide a complete governance model for managing the lifecycle of capabilities from strategic idea to completed engineering.
Portfolio Governance becomes the decision framework through which Business Owners, Platform Architects and Forge collaborate.
Forge shall recommend.
Humans shall decide.
---

Business Objective

Create a governed product development lifecycle.
Ensure that strategic decisions remain under explicit human ownership.
Provide AI-assisted portfolio management without reducing governance.
---

Business Value

Very High
Portfolio Governance enables Forge to evolve from an engineering platform into an AI-native product development platform.
Business remains responsible for strategic direction.
Forge becomes responsible for engineering execution.
---

Success Criteria

Forge supports the complete capability lifecycle.
Mission Candidates can be:
- created
- refined
- researched
- reviewed
- approved
- rejected
- archived
Mission Recommendations can be:
- proposed
- refined
- approved
- converted into Missions
Governance history remains completely traceable.

Business Approval Rationale

Engineering autonomy is valuable only when embedded within strong governance.
Portfolio Governance ensures that Forge accelerates product development while preserving human accountability, strategic direction and organisational decision making.
This Mission therefore establishes the governance foundation required for Forge to evolve into an AI-native Product Development Platform.
