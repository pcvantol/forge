# Forge Bootstrap History

## Purpose and authority

This chapter records the architectural outcome of Forge bootstrap. It is a
concise history of knowledge capture, not a replacement for the Constitution,
an implementation log, or an authority to extend the architecture.

## Bootstrap context

Forge began as a local, deterministic foundation engineered through bounded
Genesis transactions. Engineering Platform 1.5 was the temporary Bootstrap
Execution Host. It supplied the execution context but did not become the
owner of Forge knowledge, governance, product identity, or engineering
meaning.

## Established discoveries

Bootstrap established the following durable distinctions:

- a Workspace is the product boundary; a Repository is an engineering and
  evidence boundary;
- Engineering Intent is canonical while Runtime Prompts are derived;
- repository truth and evidence assess engineering outcomes;
- human governance controls approval and progression;
- capabilities are explicit, versioned units of evolution; and
- conversations and knowledge packages inform, but do not replace,
  repository-held knowledge.

These discoveries are captured in chapters 01 through 07, 09, and 10. Their
terminology is reconciled in the [Glossary](11_GLOSSARY.md); unresolved
bootstrap topics are retained in [Open Questions](12_OPEN_QUESTIONS.md).

## Completion boundary

The Bootstrap Knowledge Package is complete. Its capture does not establish a
Forge-owned Runtime Provider, Mission Runtime, execution host, API, cloud
service, marketplace, Studio, or multi-user governance implementation.

Future repository evolution derives from the Bootstrap Knowledge Package into
Repository Knowledge, then the Architecture Handbook, Engineering Intent, and
Engineering. It must not instead derive architecture from engineering
conversations.
